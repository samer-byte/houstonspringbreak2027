Week 2 FAQ

When is Week 2 of Houston Spring Break 2027?

Week 2 is March 15–21, 2027. Party nights run Thursday, March 18 to Sunday, March 21. The biggest week.

What is in the Week 2 Party Pack?

One ticket for all 9 Week 2 events: Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar (21+), Pretty Nasty, Brunch + Day Party (21+), Splash City HTX Pool Party, Houston Gone Wild, Day Party, Freaknik HTX, Day Party (21+) and March Madness (21+). It costs $150 before fees. Bought separately, the same events come to about $285.

Which schools are on Week 2?

HBCUs: Alabama A&M, Alabama State, Bowie State, Coppin State, FAMU, Morgan State and Prairie View A&M. Texas schools: UH, UT Austin, Texas A&M, Rice, TCU, SMU, Sam Houston State, Texas State, Texas Tech, UT Dallas, UTEP and UTRGV. The Week 2 flyer shows every school on the week. If your school's spring break falls in March 15–21, this is your week.

Which Week 2 events are 21+?

Thursday Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar, Friday Brunch + Day Party, Sunday Day Party and Sunday March Madness. Bring a valid government ID. Everything else in Week 2 is 18+.

How do I get the party addresses?

Addresses are announced the week of each event. They are sent to ticket holders and posted in the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event, under the refund policy on the Eventbrite listing. Request a refund from your Eventbrite order; it goes back to your original payment method.
