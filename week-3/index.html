Week 3 FAQ

When is Week 3 of Houston Spring Break 2027?

Week 3 is March 22–28, 2027. Party nights run Thursday, March 25 to Sunday, March 28. The closing week.

What is in the Week 3 Party Pack?

One ticket for all 9 Week 3 events: Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar (21+), Pretty Nasty, Brunch + Day Party (21+), Splash City HTX Pool Party, Houston Gone Wild, Day Party, Freaknik HTX, Day Party (21+) and March Madness (21+). It costs $80 before fees. Bought separately, the same events come to about $225.

Which schools are on Week 3?

HBCUs: Grambling State and Xavier (Louisiana). The Week 3 flyer shows every school on the week. If your school's spring break falls in March 22–28, this is your week.

Which Week 3 events are 21+?

Thursday Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar, Friday Brunch + Day Party, Sunday Day Party and Sunday March Madness. Bring a valid government ID. Everything else in Week 3 is 18+.

How do I get the party addresses?

Addresses are announced the week of each event. They are sent to ticket holders and posted in the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event, under the refund policy on the Eventbrite listing. Request a refund from your Eventbrite order; it goes back to your original payment method.
