Southern University FAQ

When should Southern University students come to Houston Spring Break 2027?

Southern University and A&M College is on Week 1 of the official 2027 School List, so plan on Week 1: parties run Thursday, March 11 to Sunday, March 14, 2027. The School List groups schools by spring-break week, so confirm your exact break dates on Southern University and A&M College’s official academic calendar.

What does the Week 1 Party Pack cover?

Every event in Week 1 for $80 before fees. Bought separately, the same events come to about $225. Single party tickets start at $20. Get the pack on Eventbrite.

How do I get the party addresses?

Addresses are announced the week of each event. They are sent to ticket holders and posted in the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe.

What is the age requirement?

Events are 18+ unless marked 21+. In Week 1 the 21+ events are Thursday Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar, Friday Brunch + Day Party, Sunday Day Party and Sunday March Madness.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event, under the refund policy on the Eventbrite listing. Request a refund from your Eventbrite order; it goes back to your original payment method.

Two things to do now

Grab the Week 1 pack, then join the GroupMe so you get the addresses.
