Delaware State FAQ

Which Houston Spring Break 2027 week is for Delaware State students?

Delaware State University is on Week 1 of the official 2027 School List, so plan on Week 1: parties run Thursday, March 11 to Sunday, March 14, 2027. The School List groups schools by spring-break week, so confirm your exact break dates on Delaware State University’s official academic calendar.

What does the Week 1 Party Pack cover?

Every event in Week 1 for $80 before fees. Bought separately, the same events come to about $225. Week 1 single tickets run $20–$80, and the Friday brunch is free with a ticket. Get the pack on Eventbrite.

Which Week 1 events are 21+?

Thursday Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar, Friday Brunch + Day Party, Sunday Day Party and Sunday March Madness. Bring a valid government ID. Everything else in Week 1 is 18+.

How do Delaware State students get the party addresses?

Addresses are announced the week of each event. They are sent to ticket holders and posted in the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe.

Is Houston Spring Break affiliated with Delaware State?

No. Houston Spring Break is an independent event. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Delaware State University or any other school.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event, under the refund policy on the Eventbrite listing. Request a refund from your Eventbrite order; it goes back to your original payment method.

Two things to do now

Grab the Week 1 pack, then join the GroupMe so you get the addresses.
