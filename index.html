Three Weeks In March

Houston Spring Break 2027 runs Wednesday, March 10 through Sunday, March 28, 2027 in Houston, Texas. Three weeks of mansion parties, pool parties, a booze boat party, day parties and club nights for college students and young professionals from 300+ schools, with a big HBCU turnout. Every week runs Thursday to Sunday with the same lineup, so you can plan around your own school's spring break.

Houston Spring Break FAQ

When is Houston Spring Break 2027?

Houston Spring Break 2027 runs Wednesday, March 10 through Sunday, March 28, 2027, in three weeks: Week 1 is March 8–14 (parties Thursday 3/11 to Sunday 3/14, kicking off Wednesday 3/10), Week 2 is March 15–21 (parties Thursday 3/18 to Sunday 3/21, the biggest week), and Week 3 is March 22–28 (parties Thursday 3/25 to Sunday 3/28).

Where are the Houston spring break parties?

All events are in Houston, Texas: mansion parties, pool parties, a booze boat party, day parties and club nights. Venue addresses are announced the week of each event and sent to ticket holders and the official GroupMe.

What is the age requirement?

Events are 18+ unless marked 21+. The Booze Boat Party with open bar, the Friday Brunch + Day Party, the Sunday Day Party and March Madness are 21+.

What is included in a Party Pack?

A Party Pack covers every event in one week at one price: $80 for Week 1, $150 for Week 2 and $80 for Week 3. Bought separately, the same events come to about $225 (Weeks 1 and 3) or about $285 (Week 2). Single party tickets start at $20. Prices exclude fees. Compare the packs.

How do I get the party addresses?

Addresses are announced the week of each event. They are sent to ticket holders and posted in the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe, so buy your tickets and join the GroupMe.

Can I get a VIP section or cabana?

Yes. For sections and cabanas, text 301-980-7994.

Which week should I come?

Come the week your school is on spring break. The official 2027 School List shows which week each school is on. Week 2 (March 15–21) is the biggest week.

Is Houston Spring Break for college students?

Yes. Houston Spring Break is built for college students and young professionals, with 300+ schools on the 2027 School List and a strong HBCU turnout.

How do I become a Houston Spring Break promoter?

Apply to join the promo team through the promo team application form.

Where do I buy tickets?

Tickets and Party Packs are sold on Eventbrite and Posh. Links are also on @houston_springbreak.
