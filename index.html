The official Houston Spring Break 2027 School List puts every school on the week that matches its spring break. Find your school, then lock in your week.

08 · FAQ

Houston Spring Break FAQ

When is Houston Spring Break 2027?

Houston Spring Break 2027 runs March 10–28, 2027, in three weeks, with parties Thursday to Sunday each week: Week 1 is March 8–14 (parties Thursday 3/11 to Sunday 3/14), Week 2 is March 15–21 (parties Thursday 3/18 to Sunday 3/21, the biggest week), and Week 3 is March 22–28 (parties Thursday 3/25 to Sunday 3/28).

Where are the Houston spring break parties?

All events are in Houston, Texas: mansion parties, pool parties, a booze boat party, day parties and club nights. Venue addresses are announced the week of each event and sent to ticket holders and the official GroupMe.

What is the age requirement?

Events marked 21+ are 21+; every other event is 18+. The Booze Boat Party with open bar, the Friday Brunch + Day Party, the Sunday Day Party and March Madness are 21+.

What is included in a Party Pack?

A Party Pack covers every event in one week at one price: $80 for Week 1, $150 for Week 2 and $80 for Week 3. Bought separately, the same events come to about $225 (Weeks 1 and 3) or about $285 (Week 2). Single party tickets start at $20. Prices exclude fees. Compare the packs.

How do I get the party addresses?

Addresses are announced the week of each event. They are sent to ticket holders and posted in the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe, so buy your tickets and join the GroupMe.

Can I get a VIP section or cabana?

Yes. For sections and cabanas, text 301-980-7994.

Which week should I come?

Come the week your school is on spring break. The official 2027 School List shows which week each school is on, and Find your school has a page for each HBCU and Texas school on the list. Week 2 (March 15–21) is the biggest week. Each week has its own page: Week 1, Week 2 and Week 3, and the Houston spring break guide covers the rest.

Is Houston Spring Break for college students?

Yes. Houston Spring Break is built for college students and young professionals, with 200+ schools on the 2027 School List and a strong HBCU turnout.

How do I become a Houston Spring Break promoter?

Apply to join the promo team through the promo team application form.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event, ask through your Posh order and it goes back to your original payment method.

Where do I buy tickets?

Tickets and Party Packs are sold on Posh. Links are also on @houston_springbreak.
