Houston Spring Break 2027

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026. This policy covers the Houston Spring Break app for iPhone and Android and the website houstonspringbreak2027.com.

What the app collects

  • Alerts. If you turn on alerts, the app saves your phone's push notification code and the weeks you pick (Week 1, 2 or 3). That is all. No name, phone number, email or location.
  • School search. The school you type stays on your phone. It is not sent anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.

How it is used

The alert code is used only to send you Houston Spring Break alerts: party locations the week of each event, price changes and sale days. We do not sell or share it.

Turning alerts off and deleting your data

Tap Turn off alerts in the app's Alerts tab. That deletes your alert code and weeks from our system right away. Turning off notifications in your phone's Settings also stops alerts.

Tickets

Tickets and Party Packs are sold by Posh (posh.vip). When you buy, you are on Posh's site and its own privacy policy applies. The app never sees your payment details.

Where it is stored

Alert codes are stored with Supabase, our database host, in the United States. Push alerts are delivered through Expo and Apple or Google's notification services.

Age

The app is for adults 18 and older. Some parties are 21+ and require a valid government ID.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @houston_springbreak on Instagram.
