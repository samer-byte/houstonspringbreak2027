What Houston Spring Break 2027 is

Houston Spring Break 2027 is three weeks of college parties in Houston, Texas, from March 10 to March 28, 2027: mansion parties, pool parties, a booze boat party with an open bar, day parties and club nights. It is built for college students and young professionals, with 200+ schools on the official 2027 School List and a strong HBCU turnout.

Tickets and Party Packs are sold on Eventbrite. Free RSVP on Posh saves your spot and shows who's going. Updates and addresses go out in the official GroupMe and on @houston_springbreak.

Each week follows the same pattern: four party nights, Thursday to Sunday. Come the week your school is on spring break.

How the Party Packs work

A Party Pack is one ticket for every event in one week. You buy it on Eventbrite by choosing the pack for your week.

  • Week 1 Party Pack: $80. Covers all 9 Week 1 events. Bought one by one, the same tickets come to about $225.
  • Week 2 Party Pack: $150. Covers all 9 Week 2 events. Bought one by one, the same tickets come to about $285.
  • Week 3 Party Pack: $80. Covers all 9 Week 3 events. Bought one by one, the same tickets come to about $225.
  • Single tickets start at $20, and the Friday brunch is free with a ticket.
  • Prices are before fees. Eventbrite adds its fees at checkout.

21+ rules

Events are 18+ unless marked 21+. These are 21+ every week:

  • Thursday: Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar
  • Friday: Brunch + Day Party
  • Sunday: Day Party
  • Sunday: March Madness

Bring a valid government ID to every 21+ event. For the Booze Boat, the ID is checked at the dock, boarding starts 20 minutes before departure, and the boat does not wait.

How the addresses drop

Mansion and pool party locations stay private until the week of each event. Then the addresses go to ticket holders by email and into the official Houston Spring Break GroupMe, along with day-of updates. Buy your ticket, join the GroupMe, and the address comes to you.

Getting to and around Houston

  • Two airports. Houston is served by George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and William P. Hobby (HOU). Compare flights into both.
  • Plan your rides. Rideshare apps operate across Houston. Venues are announced the week of each event, so set your rides once the addresses drop in the GroupMe.
  • Bring a valid ID. You need a valid government ID for every 21+ event.

Houston spring break FAQ

When is Houston Spring Break 2027?

March 10–28, 2027, in three weeks: Week 1 is March 8–14, Week 2 is March 15–21 and Week 3 is March 22–28. Party nights are Thursday to Sunday of each week.

How much is a Party Pack?

$80 for Week 1, $150 for Week 2 and $80 for Week 3, before fees. Each pack covers every event that week.

Are the parties 21+?

Events are 18+ unless marked 21+. The 21+ events are the Thursday Booze Boat Party w/ Open Bar, Friday Brunch + Day Party, Sunday Day Party and Sunday March Madness, every week. Bring a valid government ID.

Where are the parties?

In Houston, Texas. Venue addresses are announced the week of each event and sent to ticket holders and the official GroupMe.

Which week should I pick?

The week your school is on spring break. The Find your school page and the 2027 School List flyers show every school's week. Week 2 is the biggest week.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event, under the refund policy on the Eventbrite listing. Request a refund from your Eventbrite order; it goes back to your original payment method.

Which airports serve Houston?

Houston has two commercial airports: George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and William P. Hobby (HOU). Check both when you compare flights.
